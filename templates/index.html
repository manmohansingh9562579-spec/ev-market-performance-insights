PROJECT NOTES

Method, context and limits

Every KPI and visual uses an explicit aggregation so the numbers can be reviewed and reproduced.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

One market, multiple lenses

This project examines the Electric Vehicle Market Classification Dataset through sales, customer ratings, range, manufacturing country and battery technology. The Flask dashboard reads the training split only; the test split stays separate. Tableau Public is a separate deliverable.

METHODOLOGY

How measures are calculated

  • Sales & CO₂: sums of the supplied row values after exact-duplicate removal.
  • Ratings, range, battery capacity & Wh/km: unweighted means of non-missing record values.
  • Manufacturers & models: distinct names, not summed counts.
  • Group charts: sales sums, or category means for rating/range/efficiency.
  • Trend: annual_sales_units grouped by model_year.
LIMITATIONS

Read the totals in context

The training CSV contains one exact duplicate row, removed in memory. No missing values are imputed. The source does not establish the exact row grain or whether sales/CO₂ values overlap across records, so their sums are dataset aggregates, not independently validated market-wide totals.

Country comparisons use text labels, not geocoded boundaries. No demonstration values are presented as actual results.
