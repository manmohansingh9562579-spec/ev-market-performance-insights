PROJECT OVERVIEW

One market, multiple lenses

This dashboard examines the Electric Vehicle Market Classification Dataset through sales, customer ratings, range, manufacturing country and energy use. It reads the training split by default. The test split stays separate and is not combined into market totals.

The local Flask dashboard is an independent deliverable. It does not replace the existing Tableau workbook or a Tableau Public submission.

METHODOLOGY

How measures are calculated

  • Annual sales: sum of `annual_sales_units` across unique vehicle IDs; nulls are skipped.
  • Average rating: unweighted mean of non-null `customer_rating` values.
  • Manufacturers: distinct manufacturer names after filters.
  • CO₂ savings: sum of `co2_saved_tons_per_year` across unique vehicle IDs; nulls are skipped.
  • Category charts: sums for sales; unweighted averages for rating, range and Wh/km.
LIMITATIONS

Read the totals in context

The training CSV contains one exact duplicate vehicle ID, removed before analysis. Conflicting repeated IDs are excluded and reported. Sales and CO₂ totals are sums of the supplied row-level values; confirm the dataset's grain and field definitions before presenting them as externally validated market totals.

Country comparisons use supplied text labels and do not imply geocoded boundaries. Missing values are not imputed. No demonstration data is shown as actual data.
