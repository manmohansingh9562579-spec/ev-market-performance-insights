METHOD & SCOPE

Use the numbers
with context.

The workbook analyzes the deduplicated training split. Its measures summarize the records in this dataset; they should not be read as independently verified market-wide totals. Missing values are excluded from the applicable calculations, and aggregation follows each field's meaning.
